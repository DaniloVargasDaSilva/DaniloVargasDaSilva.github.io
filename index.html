About

I'm a Senior Software Engineer and Tech Lead at Desktop, a telecom and SaaS company in Brazil, where I lead architecture across three squads for a platform with over a million users. Most of my work sits where data, backend and product meet: an 8 TB PostgreSQL platform that replaced 400+ silos, a dispatch engine that made technician allocation 65% more efficient, and AI-assisted workflows with pre-merge evals that made my squads ship faster without regressions.

Now I'm moving toward AI engineering and forward deployed work. ConfirmaCNPJ

1day, idea to deploy

Paste a website or a CNPJ and find out whether the Brazilian company behind it is real, active and owns that site, before you pay. Firecrawl scrapes the site for the CNPJ and runs three searches in parallel; Registro.br and Federal Revenue data fill in the rest. The result is a verification slip with a trust score and the reason behind every warning. Next.js on Vercel, no database, in Portuguese and English.
